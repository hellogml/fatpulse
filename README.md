# [FatPulse](https://fatpulse.com/) — a browser-based body fat percentage calculator

FatPulse is a small health calculator tool I built to make body fat estimation less of a chore. It runs the US Navy and BMI methods side by side, so you can compare both numbers without bouncing between different apps or spreadsheet formulas.

Everything runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend server, no database, and no data upload. The measurements you enter for your neck, waist, and hips stay on your local machine and never touch the network. You just enter a few measurements, and the tool maps them against ACE ranges to show whether you land in "fitness," "acceptable," or another category, along with lean mass and fat mass estimates. You can try the [body fat calculator](https://fatpulse.com/) directly in your browser without installing anything.

Here is the shortest possible usage flow:

**Input:** neck 15 in, waist 32 in, height 70 in, male

**Output:** Navy body fat ≈ 14.2% — "Fitness" category

FatPulse converts inches into the formula automatically and displays where you sit relative to standard ranges. It also lets you target a realistic body fat percentage and work backward from a safe weekly fat-loss rate, which is useful for quick checks between milestones.
